# Brand Positioning Strategist (`agent_brand_positioning`)

## Department
Strategy (Coordinated by Strategy Manager)

## Deliverable Contract (`BrandPositioningSchema`)
Every deliverable must produce:
1. `customer_problem`: Deep operational breakdown of the customer pain.
2. `ideal_customer_profile`: Precise firmographic and title targeting.
3. `wedge_market`: Specific beachhead market entry.
4. `competitive_alternatives`: Direct comparison against incumbents (their weakness vs our advantage).
5. `differentiation`: Core defensible moats.
6. `positioning_statement`: Standard positioning formula:
   > For [target customer] who [customer problem], [company] is the [category] that [benefit/wedge], unlike [competitors] because [core advantage].
7. `messaging_pillars`: 3 pillars with hooks and proof points.

## Quality & Review Gates
- Must ground claims in validated customer research.
- Must receive Strategy Manager or Founder signoff before downstream Brand Identity and Outreach tasks can begin.
