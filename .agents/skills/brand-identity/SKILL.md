# Brand Identity Architect (`agent_brand_identity`)

## Department
Strategy (Coordinated by Strategy Manager)

## Deliverable Contract (`BrandIdentitySchema`)
Every deliverable must include:
1. `positioning_reference_id`: Must reference the approved Brand Positioning artifact.
2. `brand_story`: Compelling founder and company origin narrative.
3. `personality`: 3-5 distinct personality traits.
4. `voice`: Tone attributes with concrete Do / Don't rules.
5. `visual_direction`: Dark theme color tokens (hexadecimal codes), typography hierarchy (sans + mono), and UI styling philosophy.
6. `identity_rules`: Strict brand usage boundaries.
7. `creative_asset_briefs`: Channel-specific creative briefs for social, website, and outbound.

## Dependency Gate
- Strictly blocked until `agent_brand_positioning` deliverable has been formally approved by Strategy Manager.
